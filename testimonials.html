Data Recovery

Recovering 3 Years of University Work

The Problem

Lina's hard drive failed suddenly with no warning. Her laptop wouldn't boot and she had no backup of her university assignments, thesis drafts, and personal files.

What We Did

We ran a full drive scan, identified recoverable sectors, and extracted all intact files using professional recovery tools. The process took 18 hours.

The Result

98% of her files were successfully recovered including all university work. We also set her up with an automatic cloud backup solution going forward.

Performance

Turning a 7-Year-Old Laptop Into a Fast Machine

The Problem

Khalid's laptop took over 5 minutes to boot, froze constantly, and couldn't run basic applications. He was ready to buy a new one.

What We Did

We replaced the old HDD with a new SSD, upgraded the RAM from 4GB to 8GB, did a clean OS installation, and replaced the dried thermal paste.

The Result

Boot time dropped from 5 minutes to 18 seconds. The laptop now runs for all daily tasks. Total cost: a fraction of a new device.
