Quick Maintenance Tips

01

Restart your computer at least once a week to clear memory and install updates.

02

Keep your laptop on a hard flat surface — soft surfaces block vents and cause overheating.

03

Never charge your laptop to 100% constantly — keep it between 20% and 80% for longer battery life.

04

Use a strong unique password and enable two-factor authentication on all important accounts.

05

Clean your keyboard and vents with compressed air every few months to prevent dust buildup.

06

Uninstall programs you don't use — they slow down startup and waste storage space.
